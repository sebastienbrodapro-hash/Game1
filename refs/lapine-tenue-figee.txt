THE VELVET MUST READ AS REAL VELVET — this is the single most important rendering instruction:
- Velvet has a dense short PILE. Render the light physically: where light rakes ACROSS the pile the fabric flares to a bright silvery bloom; where the pile turns AWAY it goes to deep matte near-black that swallows light entirely. The contrast between the two is extreme and happens over a short distance.
- Every fold has a bright crushed highlight along its ridge and a dark velvety trough beside it — sharp bands of light and dark, never smooth gradients.
- The silhouette carries a soft fuzzy halo of backlit fibres, especially along the shoulders, the hip and the edge of the sleeves.
- The fabric has NO gloss and NO specular reflection like satin or leather would — it is matte and light-absorbing, glowing only through the pile.
- Close, tactile, photographic fabric behaviour. The viewer must want to touch it.
